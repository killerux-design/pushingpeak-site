What does Pushing Peak cost?

Seven ninety nine a month or forty nine ninety nine a year, and some clubs cover it for their athletes. The pricing page has the detail.

Is there a free version?

No, and we would rather say that plainly than ship a hobbled one. Downloading is free, so you can see what you are deciding about before you decide.

Where do the numbers come from?

Published sports nutrition literature, and we list every source with the values we take from it. Where the numbers come from.

Which sports is it for?

Any sport with a training schedule. Swimming, hockey, running, lifting, and the athletes doing two of those at once.

My club covers my membership. How do I join?

Ask for your team's invite code, then open Settings and Join a Team. Your access comes from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for you to buy.
