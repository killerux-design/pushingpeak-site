Support

Pushing Peak is made by a small team. Every message reaches a person, and we answer all of them, usually within one business day.

Contact us

Email: support@pushingpeak.app

To help us sort things out on the first reply, it is useful (but never required) to include the email address on your account, your device and iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

Reporting a safety concern

If someone on Pushing Peak is behaving in a way that concerns you, you can report or block them directly from their profile: open the profile, tap the … button at the top right, and choose Report or Block. Blocking takes effect immediately in both directions.

We review every report within 24 hours. If we find a violation, the reported account may be removed or restricted. You can also email us at the address above and we will treat it the same way.

How do I manage or cancel my membership?

Memberships are handled by Apple, not by us. In the app, open Settings → Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's own subscription screen. You can also reach it any time from the Settings app on your device under your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.

My club or coach covers my membership. How do I join?

Ask them for your team's invite code, then open Settings → Join a Team in the app and enter it. Your access comes from your membership in that organization, so there is nothing to buy.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Profile and tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen. This permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it. If you also hold a paid membership, cancel it separately in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Is Pushing Peak medical advice?

No. Pushing Peak provides general nutrition and training information for athletes and is not a substitute for professional medical or nutritional care. See the Terms of Service for the full disclaimer.
