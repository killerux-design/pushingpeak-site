Questions coaches ask

What does this cost my athletes?

Nothing, when your club covers membership. When it does not, athletes hold their own membership, the same as they would have anyway, and nothing changes for you either way.

What can I see about my athletes right now?

Nothing, and that is deliberate. Joining a team covers an athlete's membership, it does not open their food log to you. A coach view is coming, and athletes will be told exactly what it shows before it shows anything.

How do my athletes get started?

You email us, we send one code, and your athletes enter it under Join a Team. There is nothing to install and nothing for you to administer.

What sports is this for?

Any sport with a training schedule. If the week has hard days, easy days and competition on it, the plan follows that week.

What do you do with my athletes' data?

We do not sell it and we do not share it with data brokers. The app carries no advertising and no tracking, and it does not follow anyone across other apps or websites. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.
