You can already see who ate

You read it in the back half of a hard set. You know who skipped breakfast, who grabbed a pastry on the way in, and who is running the fourth morning of a heavy week on a cup of coffee.

You know the fix, too. You have given it a hundred times, one athlete at a time, between sets. We coach swimmers, so this is where we started as well.

That works with one athlete. It does not work with forty, and it does not work at all at five in the morning, when no coach is standing in the kitchen.

Your team is three steps from set up

  1. Tell us about your team. One email with your club and roughly how many athletes is enough to start.
  2. We set your team up and send you an invite code. One code covers your whole squad.
  3. Your athletes open Settings, then Join a Team. They enter the code and they are in. That is the whole thing.

When your club covers membership, your athletes get their access from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for them to buy. When your club does not cover it, athletes hold their own membership and there is nothing for you to set up either way.

Questions coaches ask

Do my athletes need to buy anything?

Not when your club covers membership. Their access comes from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for them to buy.

What if we cannot cover it for everyone?

Then those athletes hold their own membership, exactly as they would have anyway. You can cover some of your squad and not others, and nothing about the app changes for either group.

Is this only for swimmers?

We coach swimmers, so swimming is the sport it was built around and the one we know best. It works for any athlete training on a schedule.

How old do athletes need to be?

Thirteen and over.

What do you do with our team's data?

We do not sell your athletes' information and we do not share it with data brokers. The app carries no advertising and no tracking, and it does not follow anyone across other apps or websites. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.
