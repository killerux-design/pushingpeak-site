How a team works today

No setup on your side, and nothing for you to install.

  1. You email us about your team. Club name, sport, and roughly how many athletes is enough to start.
  2. We set the team up and send you an invite code. One code covers your whole squad.
  3. Your athletes open Settings, then Join a Team. They enter the code and they are in.

When your club covers membership, your athletes get their access from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for them to buy. When your club does not cover it, athletes hold their own membership and nothing changes for you either way.

One athlete's week

The same athlete, three different days, so you can see what the app is actually doing between your sessions.

Monday, lift at six
Something small and quick before, because there is not time to digest much. The real food lands after, inside a window while it still counts for something.
Wednesday, long run
Carbohydrate goes up the evening before, not the morning of. The plan moves it there without the athlete having to know that is the rule.
Sunday, rest
Fewer calories, and protein held where it was. This is the day athletes get wrong most often, usually by eating like it was a training day or by eating almost nothing.

Questions coaches ask

Do my athletes need to buy anything?

Not when your club covers membership. Their access comes from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for them to buy.

What if we cannot cover it for everyone?

Then those athletes hold their own membership, exactly as they would have anyway. You can cover some of your squad and not others, and nothing about the app changes for either group.

What can I see about my athletes right now?

Nothing, and that is deliberate. Joining a team covers an athlete's membership. It does not open their food log to you. When the coach view arrives we will tell athletes exactly what it shows before it shows anything, and you will know too.

What happens if an athlete leaves the club?

Their account is theirs. They keep their history and can continue with their own membership. Nothing of theirs is deleted because they left your team.

Is this only for swimmers?

No. It works for any athlete training on a schedule, in any sport. Swimming is where we coach, so it is the sport we know best and the one we started with.

How old do athletes need to be?

Thirteen and over.

What do you do with our team's data?

We do not sell your athletes' information and we do not share it with data brokers. The app carries no advertising and no tracking, and it does not follow anyone across other apps or websites. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.
