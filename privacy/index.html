Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026 (2026-08-24) Data controller: KILLERUX LLC (d/b/a Pushing Peak) Contact: kevin@killerux.design

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pushing Peak ("the app," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. KILLERUX LLC, doing business as Pushing Peak, is the controller responsible for your personal information. Pushing Peak is a sports-nutrition and training app for competitive athletes aged 13 and older.

We wrote this policy in plain language and named the actual companies that receive your data, rather than describing them as unnamed "service providers." If anything here is unclear, contact us at the address above.

Information We Collect

We collect only what the app needs to do its job. Specifically:

  • Account information: the email address you use to sign in (we authenticate with a one-time email code; we do not store a password).
  • Athlete profile: your date of birth (birthdate), height, weight, sport, and training preferences. Your birthdate is collected to compute age-appropriate fueling targets and to enforce our minimum-age policy (see Children and Minors).
  • Training and nutrition logs: the workouts, meals, and food entries you record, including the free-text meal descriptions you type and the foods you confirm.
  • Subscription and entitlement records: whether your account has active access, and the source of that access (for example, an in-app purchase).
  • Optional profile photo: if you set a profile avatar, the image you choose.
  • Blocks and reports: if you block another athlete, the block record (your account and theirs); if you report an account, the report you file, which is the reason you choose and any free-text details you add (see Blocking and Reporting below).

We do not collect precise location, health-kit data, or device advertising identifiers. We access your device contacts only on your device, to let you invite friends (see Contacts and Invites below); your address book is never uploaded to our servers and is never sold.

XP Leveling Ledger

The leveling feature (XP, Level, Rank, and streaks) records a small, behavior-only ledger of your in-app activity. Each ledger row stored in our Supabase backend contains exactly these fields:

FieldWhat it is
actionWhich fueling behavior earned XP (e.g. logging in a window, completing a plate, a streak milestone). A fixed code, never a food name
xpThe points awarded for that action (server-derived; the app never sends an amount)
multiplierThe event multiplier applied to that row (e.g. 2× during a Double-XP event), recorded as provenance
source_idAn internal reference to the log or session the earn is attributed to (an id / session code, not food content)
slot_refThe fueling slot the earn is scoped to, when applicable (a slot reference, not food content)
day_keyThe calendar day (your local day) the earn belongs to
created_atThe server timestamp when the row was written

What the XP ledger does NOT contain: the exact foods, portions, brands, or any nutritional content you log. Those never leave your device as part of the leveling feature. Only the behavioral rows above are synced. This is a hard data-split invariant of the design.

No new data recipient. The XP ledger is stored in the same Supabase backend that already processes your account and coaching data. Enabling leveling does not introduce any new third party or data recipient, and the app uses no third-party analytics SDK for XP telemetry; calibration is performed with SQL views over the existing ledger.

Public-by-default social visibility. Pushing Peak is a social app. Your profile is public by default to other athletes on the app. This includes your profile photo, display name, username, streaks, XP, Level, and Rank. Other athletes can follow you and compare progress. In this version there is no private-account option and no visibility toggle: if you use the app, this profile information is visible to other athletes. Following is one-directional and comparative only. There is no direct messaging in Pushing Peak, so other athletes cannot send you private messages.

Contacts and Invites

Pushing Peak lets you invite friends from your device's address book. This happens entirely on your device. When you tap Invite, your phone opens its own Messages or Mail composer, pre-addressed to the friend you chose, and the invitation is sent from your own account. Your address book (and any hash, copy, or derivative of it) is never uploaded to Pushing Peak's servers, and is never sold or shared with anyone. We do not match your contacts against our users, and we do not use your contacts for any purpose other than the single invite you choose to send. Nothing about your contacts ever leaves your device.

Blocking and Reporting

Because profiles are public by default, Pushing Peak lets you block and report other athletes. Both records are stored in the same Supabase backend that already holds your account data (see Third Parties We Share Data With); they are not shared with any third party and add no new data recipient.

  • Blocks. When you block an athlete, we store a block record containing your account id and the blocked account's id, with a timestamp. A block is symmetric: after you block someone, neither of you can find the other in search, suggestions, follower lists, or on a profile page, regardless of who pressed Block. The block record persists until you unblock that account (or until either account is deleted). Unblocking removes only your own block; a block the other person placed on you stays in place.
  • Reports. When you report an account, we store your account id (as the reporter), the reported account's id, the reason you selected, the report's review status, and any free-text details you typed (up to 1,000 characters). Report details are your own words about another person, and we (the operator) read them in order to review the report; our team reviews every report within 24 hours. A report is retained as a safety record even if you, the reporter, later delete your account: the report itself is kept, with your reporter id detached from it (see Data Retention).

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and secure your account; calculate day-aware macro and fueling targets from your profile and training; identify and ground the foods you log against trusted nutrition databases; improve the accuracy of that food matching over time; sync your data across your devices; manage your subscription and access; and respond to support requests. We do not use your information for advertising or for building profiles about you across other apps or websites.

Photos and Images

  • Barcode scanning happens on your device. When you scan a product barcode, your camera reads the barcode locally on your phone. The image is not uploaded; only the decoded barcode number is used to look up the product.
  • Profile avatar. If you set a profile photo, it is stored in Supabase (above).

The app does not send meal photos to any third party for AI food identification; food logging uses the text you type, not images.

No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Pushing Peak contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share any data for behavioral or targeted advertising. Any usage signals we keep are first-party and stay within our own Supabase database. This absence is deliberate and is part of how we protect the athletes who use it.

How Third Parties Protect Your Data

Each company listed above is bound by its own agreement and privacy commitments to provide protection for your data that is at least equivalent to the protection described in this policy, and to process the data only for the purposes we specify. We share only the data each recipient needs for its stated function.

Data Retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide the app and to meet our legal obligations. Your profile, logs, and account data are retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your associated personal information from our systems, and we instruct our processors to do the same, except where we are required to retain limited records (for example, transaction records) to comply with law. We do not retain children's personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected.

AI food-logging accuracy records. To find and fix wrong food matches, we keep a server-side record of each AI meal-log request: the meal description you typed, any correction you gave, the foods the app matched, and which match was shown first. These records stay in our own Supabase database (they are not sent to any additional third party), are used only to improve the accuracy of the app's food matching and AI logging, and are deleted automatically after 90 days, or immediately if you delete your account.

One safety exception: abuse reports you filed about other accounts are retained after your account is deleted. The report (reason, status, and any free-text details) is kept as a safety record, with your reporter id detached from it, so that a report cannot be erased by deleting the account that filed it. Reports filed about you, and your block records, are deleted along with your account.

Children and Minors

Pushing Peak is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly permit accounts for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. The app is directed to competitive teen and adult athletes; it is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact kevin@killerux.design and we will act to remove it. Where the app is available to minors 13 and older, we honor the age and consent signals the app store provides.

Your Choices and Rights

You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time, and you can delete your account, which removes your associated personal information as described under Data Retention. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, port, or delete your personal information; to exercise them, contact kevin@killerux.design.

Data Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to safeguard your information and to limit who can access it.

International Users

We operate from the United States, and your information is processed in the United States and in the regions our processors operate. By using the app you understand that your information may be processed in the United States.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves or as the law requires. When we make a material change, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
