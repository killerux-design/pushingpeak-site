Getting your squad on it

Nothing to install, and nothing to set up on your side.

  1. You email us. Club, sport, and roughly how many athletes.
  2. We set the team up and send you an invite code. One code covers the whole squad.
  3. Your athletes open Settings, then Join a Team. They enter the code and they are in.

When your club covers membership, athletes get their access from membership in the organization, so there is nothing for them to buy. When it does not, they hold their own membership and nothing changes for you either way.

Questions coaches ask

Is the free year really free?

Yes, and there is nothing to cancel afterwards that you did not choose. We apply it by hand to the account you tell us about.

What can I see about my athletes right now?

Nothing, and that is deliberate. Joining a team covers an athlete's membership. It does not open their food log to you. When the coach view arrives, athletes will be told exactly what it shows before it shows anything.

What if my club cannot cover everyone?

Then those athletes hold their own membership, as they would have anyway. You can cover some of your squad and not others, and the app is identical for both.

What happens if an athlete leaves the club?

Their account is theirs. They keep their history and carry on with their own membership. Nothing is deleted because they left your team.

Is this only for swimmers?

No. It works for any athlete training on a schedule, in any sport. Swimming is where we coach, so it is the one we know best and where we started.

What do you do with our data?

We do not sell your athletes' information and we do not share it with data brokers. The app carries no advertising and no tracking, and it does not follow anyone across other apps or websites. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.
