Questions worth asking

How is this different from a calorie tracker?

A tracker records what you already ate. This builds the day before you eat it, around the sessions you have. Both are useful. Only one of them helps at five in the morning. We wrote the long version in an honest comparison.

Which sports is it for?

Any sport with a training schedule. Swimming, hockey, running, lifting, and the athletes doing two of those at once.

Where do the numbers come from?

Public food databases, and the app shows you which one and lets you correct it. The recommendations have sources too, and they are all listed, with the actual values.

What do you do with my data?

We do not sell it and we do not share it with data brokers. There is no advertising and no tracking in the app, and it does not follow you across other apps or websites. The detail is in the Privacy Policy.

My club or coach covers my membership. How do I join?

Ask them for your team's invite code, then open Settings and Join a Team. More for coaches and teams.
