Questions

Is there a free version?

There is no free tier, and we would rather say that plainly than ship a hobbled one. Your first week is free, so you get the whole app before you decide about it. After that it needs a membership, or a club covering it.

What do I get for it?

All of it. Every meal with its own time window and targets, the day rebuilding around the training you add, food you can describe in your own words, and every number traceable to published work. There is nothing held back for a higher tier, because there is no higher tier.

How do I cancel?

In the app, open Settings and then Manage Subscription, which opens Apple's own screen. You can also reach it from the Settings app on your device under your Apple Account. Cancelling stops the next renewal and access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.

What happens to my history if I stop?

It stays. Your account and your data are yours, and stopping a membership does not delete either. If you want the account itself removed, Support explains how, and that one is permanent.

Who is it for?

Athletes aged thirteen and over, in any sport with a training schedule. It is built around fuelling performance rather than changing your weight, which is a deliberate choice and shapes everything the app recommends.
